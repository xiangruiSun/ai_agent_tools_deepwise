TASK 10 - TRAINING DATA GOVERNANCE
===================================
Deepwise focus: model training data.

Job
---
Before any image or label reaches a training set, prove it is de-identified, selected by an
explicit cohort definition, labelled with evidence, and that disagreements go to people.

Requirements
------------
R1  De-identification audit (deid_audit, dwagent/ct/store.py). Samples the DICOM headers of a
    study and reports which identifying fields are populated (name, birth date, address,
    physician names, institution, accession number, device serial number), the
    PatientIdentityRemoved flag, the de-identification method, burned-in annotation flag and the
    number of private tags. It returns field names and counts only, never values.
R2  Cohort builder (build_cohort). A request in words is turned into a validated CohortQuery
    (size range, slice thickness, contrast, reader agreement, texture, malignancy rating) by
    structured output; the query is executed by code against the annotation database of all
    1,018 LIDC scans. The model never writes SQL and never counts.
    The generation schema (CohortQueryStrict) makes every field required, and business rules
    (governance.cohort_rules) reject a query that omits something the request names, such as
    the nodule texture or the contrast condition; the errors go back through the repair loop.
    Measured reason: with optional fields the model returned an empty query for all 5 requests,
    and with required fields it still left texture null in 3 of 5 until the rule was added
    (verification/t10_before_cohort_rules.json keeps that run).
    Size filtering reads the contour table once and caches it; per-annotation queries on the
    unindexed table took minutes and timed out the tool.
R3  Label extraction (dwagent/governance.py). Structured labels from published case text:
    nodule or mass present, largest lesion in mm, laterality, modalities, malignancy confirmed,
    and an evidence quote. Accepted only if anchored: the quote is an exact substring of the
    text and the size is a number in that quote (centimetres converted). Unanchored labels are
    routed to human review, not to the training set.
R4  Independent cross-check: a model-free regular expression extracts sizes from the same text.
R5  Hard-example queue (detector_vs_readers). Per study: nodules the readers marked and the
    detector missed, with size and type, for the next training round.
R6  Dataset overview: local studies by size band, bytes, licence, and annotation-database counts.
R7  Authorization. These tools need the dataset:read scope, held by company staff. Staff have no
    access to patient reports or images through any tool or route (task 13).
R8  Licence tracking. Each dataset's licence is recorded in data/README.md and in the manifest.
    PMC-Patients is non-commercial; it may be used for this research demo and not in a product.

Verification on real data
-------------------------
python scripts/verify.py t10    ->  verification/t10_training_data.json
  a. De-identification audit of all 10 studies.
  b. Five cohort requests in words; for each, the parsed query against a hand-written query and
     the resulting scan counts against the hand-written query's counts.
  c. Label extraction on 12 real PMC-Patients summaries: accepted or sent to review, attempts,
     and whether the regular expression finds the same size.
  d. The hard-example queue over the 10 studies.

Known limits
------------
The audit reads header fields and flags; it does not look for text burned into pixels. The
cohort size filter uses the largest in-plane contour extent of an annotation, an approximation
of nodule diameter. Labels are checked for anchoring, not for clinical correctness.
