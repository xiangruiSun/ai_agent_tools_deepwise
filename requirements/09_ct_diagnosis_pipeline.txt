TASK 09 - CT DIAGNOSIS PIPELINE
================================
Deepwise focus: the CT diagnosis system. Uses the runtime of task 03 and the validation of task 02.

Job
---
Take a chest CT series from the archive to a signed report: check the acquisition, run the
detector, have the candidates reviewed, categorise the confirmed findings, draft, flag critical
results, and stop for the radiologist.

Requirements
------------
R1  Archive access (dwagent/ct/store.py). List studies; read DICOM headers; load a series as a
    Hounsfield-unit volume sorted by slice position with spacing; cache volumes.
R2  Acquisition gate. Analysis runs only when the series is CT, slice thickness <= 2.5 mm, no
    gaps between slices, at least 60 slices. Contrast enhancement raises a warning that is
    carried into the report limitations.
R3  Detector interface. run_nodule_detector(patient_id) returns ranked candidates with location,
    long and short axis, mean diameter, density and a score. The implementation here,
    classical_v1.1 (dwagent/ct/detector.py), is a rule-based stand-in for a registered model:
    lung segmentation by thresholding, compact dense objects inside the lungs, a second pass
    that opens each slice to detach large masses from vessels, at most 12 candidates per scan.
R4  Measurement (dwagent/ct/geometry.py). Long and short axis from the principal axes of the
    region on its largest slice; mean diameter is their average to one decimal. The same code
    measures detector masks and reader contours.
R5  Review. Detector candidates are never reported directly. get_confirmed_findings returns the
    reviewed list. In this demo the review is SIMULATED from the LIDC reader annotations: a
    candidate matching an annotated nodule is confirmed, others are rejected, and annotated
    nodules the detector missed are added. In a hospital this step is a person.
R6  Categorisation (dwagent/ct/lungrads.py). Deterministic Lung-RADS v2022 for solid, part-solid
    and non-solid nodules at baseline and follow-up, benign calcification, the 4X modifier, the
    exam category and the management text. The language model never computes a category.
R7  Draft. One schema-constrained model call writes the report text from the facts; the result
    must pass the business rules of task 02; otherwise the deterministic template is used and
    the report says so.
R8  Normal studies. No confirmed findings: template report, exam category 1, no model call.
R9  Critical results. Exam category 4B or 4X sets the critical flag shown at sign-off.
R10 Sign-off. The run pauses; a radiologist approves or rejects; only a signed report is visible
    to the patient; a newer signed report supersedes the older one.

Simplifications, stated
-----------------------
Lung-RADS is a screening system and LIDC-IDRI is not a screening cohort: it is used here to
exercise the rules, not to make screening claims. All studies are treated as baseline (LIDC has
no priors). Airway nodules, atypical cysts and the step-down of stable category 3 and 4A nodules
are not modelled. Zone is upper, mid or lower third of the lung extent, not the anatomical lobe.
The solid component of a part-solid nodule is estimated from voxels above -300 HU.

Verification on real data
-------------------------
python scripts/verify.py t09    ->  verification/t09_ct_diagnosis.json
  a. Detector against the four-reader annotations on all 10 studies: nodules, detected, missed
     (with size and type), false positives per scan, runtime.
  b. Lung-RADS boundary table: 19 cases at every threshold, including follow-up and modifier
     rules; all must match.
python scripts/verify.py t03    all 10 studies end to end (see task 03)
Reader contour orientation was checked on the data: mean HU at contour centres is -168 read as
(column, row) and -406 the other way, so (column, row) is used.

Known limits
------------
The detector is a baseline, not a product: it misses pure ground-glass nodules by design and has
about ten false positives per scan. It exists so the agents meet a real algorithm with real
latency and real errors.
