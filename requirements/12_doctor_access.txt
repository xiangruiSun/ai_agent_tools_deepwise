TASK 12 - DOCTOR ACCESS
========================
Deepwise focus: doctor access.

Job
---
Give the radiologist a worklist, an image viewer, the study pipeline with sign-off, and a
copilot that answers questions from tools and cites what it used.

Requirements
------------
R1  Worklist. Every study with slice count, thickness, report status (none, draft, signed) and
    exam category.
R2  Study view. Windowed CT images with a slider; confirmed findings circled on their slice;
    acquisition check; the findings table with size, type, location and whether each came from
    the detector or was added by a reader.
R3  Run the pipeline from the study view; see the nodes as they complete, the draft source
    (model, template, template after failed validation), the critical flag and the trace.
R4  Sign-off queue. Runs paused at a checkpoint, each with its draft; approve and sign, or
    reject. The decision resumes the run. The agent can never sign: sign_report requires an
    approval record created by a doctor's action in the portal.
R5  Copilot (dwagent/profiles.py DOCTOR). Tools: list_ct_studies, get_series_metadata,
    check_series_quality, run_nodule_detector, get_confirmed_findings, lung_rads_category,
    search_guideline, search_similar_cases, get_report, calculator, the assess_nodules skill, and
    remember.
R6  Copilot rules: facts only from tools; categories only from the Lung-RADS tool or the skill;
    detector candidates are described as unreviewed; guideline and case ids are cited; a failed
    tool is reported, not papered over; the grounding guard of task 01 checks every answer.
R7  Similar cases come from published case reports and are labelled as such, never presented as
    this hospital's patients.
R8  Role separation. A doctor account can read every study and report, draft and sign. It has no
    access to operations, audit or dataset-governance tools.
R9  Every answer shows its trace: model calls, tool calls with inputs and outputs, latency,
    tokens, retries, and why the run stopped.

Code
----
dwagent/profiles.py, dwagent/web/app.py (/api/worklist, /api/studies, /api/approvals, /api/chat)
dwagent/web/static/app.js (pageWorklist, pageStudy, pageApprovals, pageChat)

Verification on real data
-------------------------
python scripts/verify.py t12    ->  verification/t12_doctor_access.json
  Five real questions through the chat path: a critical-result assessment, a study with no
  findings, "which finding drives the exam category", a guideline question about a new 5 mm
  nodule, and similar published cases for a real study. Recorded: tools used, whether the
  expected fact is in the answer, tokens, time.
Browser check on the running portal (2026-11-18): worklist of 10 studies, study view with image
and findings, pipeline run to "waiting for you", approval, report signed and then visible in the
patient account.

Known limits
------------
The viewer shows one axial series in a fixed lung window, with no measurement tools. The
copilot's similar-case search is lexical.
