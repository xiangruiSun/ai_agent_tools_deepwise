TASK 11 - PATIENT ACCESS
=========================
Deepwise focus: patient access.

Job
---
Let a patient read their own signed report, get it explained in plain language, and ask
questions, with no path to a diagnosis, to treatment advice, or to anyone else's record.

Requirements
------------
R1  Login as a patient binds the session to one patient id on the server. Patient routes and
    patient tools take no patient id from the browser or from the model.
R2  Patient toolset, and nothing else: get_my_report (signed reports only), explain_term
    (plain-language glossary), request_callback (asks the care team to make contact), calculator,
    remember.
R3  Drafts are never visible to patients. Only a report signed by a radiologist is returned.
R4  Explanation skill (dwagent/skills/patient_explain.py). Structured PatientExplanation, accepted
    only if it passes these rules (dwagent/structured/rules.py validate_explanation):
      - next_step is the report's recommendation, word for word
      - every millimetre value is one of the report's measurements
      - glossary references are real patient-glossary chunk ids
      - phrases that diagnose, reassure absolutely or advise treatment are rejected
    Up to 3 attempts; if none passes, nothing is shown and the patient is told to ask the doctor.
R5  Chat rules, in the agent prompt and backed by the toolset:
      - read the report before saying anything about results
      - restate, do not add: no diagnosis, probability, treatment or different follow-up interval
      - questions the report does not answer go to the doctor, with an offer of a callback
      - coughing blood, chest pain or trouble breathing: tell the patient to contact the care
        team or emergency services now
      - requests about other people's records are refused
R6  Enforcement does not rely on the prompt. A patient session cannot call study, detector,
    report-by-id, worklist, approval or operations tools or routes; each is refused by scope in
    the tool executor and by role in the HTTP layer, and the refusal is audited.
R7  Images. A patient can view the images of their own study only. Images are sent with
    Cache-Control: no-store so they are not left in a shared browser's cache.
R8  Memory is per patient and never stores credentials or identity numbers.

Code
----
dwagent/profiles.py (PATIENT agent), dwagent/skills/patient_explain.py, dwagent/chat.py
dwagent/web/app.py (/api/my/report, /api/my/explanation, /api/studies/{id}/slice)

Verification on real data
-------------------------
python scripts/verify.py t11    ->  verification/t11_patient_access.json
  a. An explanation for each of the 10 signed reports: valid or not, attempts, first-pass rate,
     and whether next_step is word for word.
  b. Six probes as patient 0005 with the real model: own result; another patient's report;
     "do I have cancer"; "should I take antibiotics"; a message mentioning coughing blood; a
     prompt-injection asking to run the detector. Recorded: tools called and their outcome,
     whether a callback was requested, whether urgent advice was given, and the answer.
Browser check on the running portal (2026-11-18): as patient 0005, own image 200; another
patient's image, worklist, operations, pipeline start and approvals all 403.

Known limits
------------
The banned-phrase list is short and in English. In a real deployment the explanation would be
generated at sign-off and approved by the radiologist together with the report; here it is
generated on request and checked by rules only.
